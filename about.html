About

Hey! I'm Franchesca Bethel

I've been putting people together since I was eight years old with a sewing machine and way too much confidence. Monet's Styling is that same love, all grown up.

B.S. Fashion Merchandising & Retailing MBA, Operations & Supply Chain

“The past is your lesson. The present is your gift. The future is your motivation.”

— Zig Ziglar

I grew up glued to Say Yes to the Dress and Project Runway, completely obsessed with the moment a look finally comes together. I got my first sewing machine at eight years old, and it didn't sit idle — I was sewing sample pieces for a friend and stitching my own cheer team's uniforms before I could even drive.

Some of my favorite memories are sitting on the floor of my mother's, grandmother's, and aunt's closets, admiring every piece and mentally styling outfits for every occasion I could think of. I was always the “extra” one — no matter what the dress code said, I found a way to take it up a notch and dress to impress.

That love for putting people together eventually became my career. I hold a B.S. in Fashion Merchandising and Retailing and an MBA in Operations and Supply Chain Management, and I've spent the last five-plus years leading and styling with intention. But at the core, Monet's Styling is still that girl with the sewing machine — I just get to do it for you now.

I consider myself a “Direction Setter” — the kind of person who sees a picture of a desired future and does whatever it takes to get there. Whatever your version of your best-dressed, most confident self looks like, I want to help you get there — one closet, one outfit, one occasion at a time.
